# Annual Wikipedia module source and series review

**Verdict:** conditional acceptance of the raw assertion transcription and source named history series association, with all source quality and historical continuity limits intact.

The recovered rows preserve the integer year/value, raw source key or inline source text, date note, citation kind, and module/source locator. In the fixed sample, the years, population strings, and keyed/inline citation text matched the frozen Lua rows. The exact article comment was also spot checked against cached API title/page/revision; the sampled Wikidata article URL, QID, explicit P764 claim and exact current source name/type/region/code chain matched. The binding is good evidence that the row belongs to the Wikipedia article's history for the currently named settlement entry.

This review does not establish that each cited work supports its displayed number. The citations were not fetched. Scope and exactness remain unknown, and historical continuity to a physical site or census-date polygon is not established. Empty and unresolved citations remain visible as such. Six conflicting key/year assertions stay held.

The staged dataset has 34,004 secondary assertions linked to 7,716 candidate entries over 241 observed years (1150–2025), and admits zero place identities, population values, or spatial records. Comparisons are diagnostics: 1,535 observations reach accepted 2002/2010 identity graph endpoints, 992 are compared with their current 2021 source row, and 5,544 are exact normalized-name/type/region diagnostics only. The latter are not identity links. The 3,706 confidentiality-perturbed differences are protected comparisons. Equality and disagreement alike do not resolve scope, date, or boundary comparability, and official census values are not replaced.
