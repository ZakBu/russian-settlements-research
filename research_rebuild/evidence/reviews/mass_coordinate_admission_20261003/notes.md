Missing provider query receipts limit replay and observation-date traceability.

Independently recomputed ADM1 membership found all 205 provider sample points and all 204 sampled Wikidata candidate point records inside their expected 2017 ADM1 polygons. That is a regional consistency check, not a settlement footprint check.

The C city rule is supported for physical city source rows with unique exact P764/code, exact Russian label, physical type path and an unambiguous point. Eleven historical points are 0.29–4.04 km from the Wikidata point and corroborate those new point claims. This does not imply a current city polygon or unchanged boundaries.
