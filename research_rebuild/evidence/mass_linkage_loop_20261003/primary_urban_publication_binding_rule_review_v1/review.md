# Independent review: same-census 2010 PGT publication binding

**Verdict: approve the 780 exact old-to-new pairs in the pinned proposal for same-census 2010 Table 5 population binding.** This admits a primary publication value for the matched 2010 source record. It does not establish settlement identity across years, a boundary history, or a district assignment. Keep the prior R2 assertion and its original quality tag alongside the Table 5 observation; the later population application remains a separate review.

The rule is sufficient for ordinary PGT binding without a full individual historical-district dossier because the positive claim is narrow and directly checked: the selected 2010 R2 row is re-read from a pinned workbook at its cited row; raw type, name, region and population match; its normalized region/name/type key is unique across the complete selected 2010 source domain; and one unique physical Table 5 locality row has the same key, a direct PGT label, a numeric population and matching pypdf and Poppler text. I independently recomputed both whole-domain key counts over all 943 rows: 901 are unique in R2 and 901 in physical Table 5. The staged uniqueness flags match those recomputations. All 780 proposed old IDs resolve to selected rows and overlap records, all 780 new IDs resolve to extracted physical Table 5 settlement rows, and the IDs are one-to-one. The two same-level explicit district conflicts are excluded.

I re-read a fixed 60-row sample from 17 source workbooks, comprising the 30 largest absolute deltas and 30 rows drawn with seed `20261003` across region-by-district-evidence strata. Workbook bytes matched the recorded source hash; every sampled raw label, population and region matched its selected R2 record. I also re-extracted each cited official PDF line with pypdf and Poppler. All 60 lines were at the cited page and line, had the expected PGT label and numeric population, and each Poppler match was unique. The sample spans 50 PDF pages. This is an audit sample, not a statistical accuracy estimate; row-level raw workbook and PDF checks for the unsampled proposals rely on the producer's pinned per-row gate outputs.

The PGT parser accepts the documented `рп`, `кп` and `дп` annotations while retaining the PGT type. In the 780 approved rows these occur 199, 6 and 1 times respectively; the other 574 have no suffix. The sample directly rechecked the raw label and printed locality line. Do not generalize the rule to ambiguous labels, duplicate keys, grouped/aggregate lines or unmatched type/name variants.

All 780 old values carry `confidentiality_perturbed_within_ten`; all have `population_scope=settlement`. The legacy `entity_grain_status` field is blank for all 780, so the row-level grain claim rests on the re-read typed PGT source label and the direct typed physical PGT line in Table 5, not that metadata field. Preserve this limitation. The old-to-new difference is not explained by this binding review: 294 absolute differences exceed 10, 126 exceed 100 and 37 exceed 1,000; the largest is 4,063. Those deltas do not prove a wrong binding, but they also must not be described as ordinary within-ten corrections. Keep old populations, tags and both assertions inspectable and do not allocate the aggregate delta or infer its cause.

Тура and Диксон remain held. Their selected R2 rows carry explicit Эвенкийский and Таймырский district values while the pre-existing parsed Table 5 district is Шушенский; PDF context shows special-status district headers around the proper locality lines, but that context has not been converted into a proven row-level district binding. Do not release those IDs based only on this review.
