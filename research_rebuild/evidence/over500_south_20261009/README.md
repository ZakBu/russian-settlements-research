# South >500 source packet, 2026-10-09

Frozen stage71 inputs; 173 assigned residual source records in 22 regions. This packet admits 32 own locality point uses, 18 effective identity edges, and three typed secondary-source inclusion events. Ordinary identity and inclusion are separate relations. Literal census parts stay typed holds and receive no whole locality point projection.

The stable identity rules use unique native name/type/county, physically reopened two-sided source brackets, explicit source-declared alternative names, or unique names across all native types in each region/year with transparent printed station/designator normalization. Missing county context may be accepted under the all-type unique regional rule; contradictory known counties and repeated census-year alternatives block acceptance. Population similarity and provider quality do not establish identity.

Own points are transferred from a frozen accepted own-point donor over proved identity, independently bound to an own Wikipedia article by literal name/alias and county/type or printed former-city administrative header, or bound to a unique 2009 classifier physical settlement and the same own typed raw GeoKLADR 2011 code/name. Native population cells and row names were reopened. Historical native codes, census-date point measurement, modern boundary harmonization and population comparability are not asserted.

The inclusion events are Колос and Даниловка to Шахты in 2004, and Винтай to Самара in 2005. Own article literal dates are admitted at year precision, with cited primary act dates retained as secondary citations. Primary acts were not reopened. Actual receiving-city observations in all three years are context only; no receiving-city population or point is assigned to a child. No ordinary union or closed receiving-city roster is asserted.

Explicit holds include indistinguishable 2010 Черноярская station labels, multiple old own-code alternatives, and the contradictory accepted 2010/current Инхело points. The Горная Поляна article in Ленинский район is a different locality from the former locality below the Волгоград city source header. Unobserved/unknown counts remain unknown.

`dispositions.csv` covers every assigned source ID. `manifest.json` pins inputs, accepted outputs and builders. Raw source captures supplied by `over500_south_wiki_sources_20261009` are separately pinned. No frozen source, loader or Git state was edited.
