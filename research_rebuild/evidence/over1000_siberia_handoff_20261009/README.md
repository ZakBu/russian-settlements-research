# Siberian handoff: Krasnoyarsk, Irkutsk, and Zabaikalsky (13 assigned UIDs)

This packet handles the handoff roster from `over1000_root_20261009/handoff_bash_agent_regional269_roster.csv`. It reuses the frozen Usолье-7 version 27 and East Amithasha version 29 evidence packages without copying or re-adjudicating them. The other 11 UIDs are supported by native source rows, exact locality articles, source-order context, alias/type transitions, or typed lifecycle/publication evidence. The raw counts and quality labels are copied unchanged.

Positive group paths are: Kежма (2002/2010 observed, inundated in 2012 and abolished in 2014); Кеуль (2002/2010 observed, abolished under Irkutsk Law 124-OZ in 2016); station Ага (2002/2010 alias/2021 native); Канский Таёжный → Таёжное (dated 2003 type conversion); Краснокаменский Октябрьский (2002/2010 observed, evacuation and 2014 abolition); and Калганская Кадая (2002 pgt, dated 2008 village status, 2010/2021). Чистые Ключи has a 2021 own article/native point and no separate row in the selected full 2002/2010 register files; the article says the military settlement existed from 1972, so publication nonappearance is not physical absence and no earlier population is assigned.

The exact 13-UID × 3-year ledger is in `source_year_credit_ledger.csv`. Event-closed and selected-register nonappearance slots do not receive a zero or estimated population. Existing accepted coordinates for Kежма, Кеуль, station Ага, Таёжное, Калганская Кадая, and Чистые Ключи are retained; page-point cross-checks and their distances are in `existing_point_article_corroboration.csv`. New points are limited to missing old/source UIDs for Taezhny, Kaday, old Oktjabrsky rows, and the 2010 Aга alias.

`own_article_revision_witnesses.csv` and the gzip API response pin the exact live article revisions and their points/status text. `native_source_row_witnesses.csv` retains source file hashes, row locators, raw cell values, native values, and hierarchy context. The nonappearance scan applies only to the three checked register files named in its receipt. It does not claim physical absence, restricted population attribution, or a census-date coordinate.
