# RSFSR 1989 rural settlement evidence: Volga and selected regions

This is source-native evidence, not an accepted population table. No coordinates, integrated records, Git state, sparse artifacts, or frozen outputs were changed. All extracted numbers refer to actual named settlements; no district or region total was distributed among villages.

## Usable extracts

- `tatarica_rural_1989.csv`: completed bulk first pass of the Institute of Tatar Encyclopedia and Regional Studies, Academy of Sciences of the Republic of Tatarstan. All 43 district article catalogs enumerated through the public UI's article-list endpoint, then exact 1989 values extracted from the “Число жителей” / population sections of rural settlement articles. Official book Volume 1 PDF page 3 states that settlement population time series are based on official population censuses; the exception stated there concerns 2015 council data. PDF source: https://tatarica.org/download_file/view/11463 (redirects to https://tatarica.org/application/files/1316/0837/6920/NP_1.pdf). SHA256: `3415ab668c1ee8f220821e813bc3c3756c65665a90523ed163659dcccb825fd0`.
- `bashenc_rural_1989.csv`: bounded partial bulk extraction from the Bashkir Encyclopedia's public 1989-filtered rural article catalogs. Only exact person counts, and only positively identified Bashkortostan districts, are retained. Rounded counts in thousands, current/historical urban duplicates, and potentially combined settlements are held. Census attribution is inferred from the population sequence; a source-methodology statement for these specific online articles has not been established. Treat this source as secondary historical numbers requiring that remaining provenance check.
- `altai_zonal_rural_1989.csv`: 15 individually named rural settlements, 13,600 people, from Table 12 of the official 2011 Zonal district territorial plan, mirrored at https://base.garant.ru/7339296/. Source states population census data. The HTML's fine-grained colspans are preserved in `altai_zonal_table12_cells.json`. Four numeric cells merged across 1989 and 2002 are held rather than assumed to apply individually to either year. Шубенка, already excluded because of its merged numeric cell, is also flagged because its explicit settlement count (1,355) exceeds the source’s own сельсовет control total (1,335); no correction is guessed. Соколово is explicitly a pgt in 1989 and excluded.

Each encyclopedia CSV preserves source URL, SHA256, archived HTML path, source-native subtitle and population sequence, census year, count basis, grain, license status, and review status. `extract_summary.json` holds final extract row counts and SHA256s. `source_exclusions.json` preserves urban, ambiguous district, combined-population, and conflicting alias exclusions. These sums are source coverage, not added coverage after matching to existing records.

## Count-basis limit

Presence versus permanent residence is not specified in the encyclopedia articles or the TATARICA methodological paragraph. Example: TATARICA gives Базарные Матаки 4,175 in 1989; Demoscope's present-population district-center table gives 4,257. Prefer the explicit primary count where sources duplicate a settlement, and retain source-specific basis rather than equating the figures. 1989 urban exclusions are based on the already archived Demoscope `rus89_reg2.php` census transcription; normalized exclusion names are retained in `urban_exclusion_names.json`.

## Raw evidence and reproduction

Raw downloads are in `/workspace/shared/rsfsr1989_rural_volga`. Download manifests are `tatarica_download_manifest.jsonl`, `tatarica_category_manifest.json`, `bashenc_download_manifest.jsonl`, and `additional_sources_manifest.json`. Category manifest requests retain the exact public endpoint, POST form, district identity, response hash, and response bytes. Source copyrights and absent open licenses are recorded. The analytical CSVs extract factual numerical observations, rather than republishing narrative articles or books. The source materials themselves remain archived evidence.

## Exact source availability limits

- Bashkortostan: an actual all-settlement book exists: *Населённые пункты Башкортостана. 1979 и 1989 годы*, volume III, Ufa, Kitap, 2018. The official statistics bulletin describes it: https://02.rosstat.gov.ru/storage/mediabank/LDrWi9z2/Byulleten%27-vypusk-%E2%84%961.pdf. Archive holdings are described at https://kumertau-archive.ru/новости/кумертауведение-online/982-архивная-библиотека-«населенные-пункты-башкортостана».html. No accessible full scan was obtained in this pass. A downloaded historical monograph `bash_history_2006.pdf` has historical tables but was not admitted as an extracted 1989 dataset.
- Altai Krai: the genuine primary regional census publication is *Сельские населенные пункты Алтайского края (по данным Всесоюзной переписи населения 1989 года)*, Barnaul, 1990, 385 pages. Confirmed by Gorno-Altaisk State University library catalog and scholarly bibliographies; no accessible full scan obtained. The downloaded 2024 settlement dissertation only has its 1989-urban-settlement appendix as accessible named population data; these were not admitted as rural additions. Its maps do not expose the complete rural numerical table.
- Voronezh: the primary book *Сельские населенные пункты Воронежской области (по данным Всесоюзной переписи населения 1979 г. и 1989 г.)*, Voronezh, 1990, 78 pages, is cited in Krupko's rural settlement dissertation bibliography: https://www.dissercat.com/content/territorialnaya-organizatsiya-selskogo-naseleniya-i-rasseleniya-voronezhskoi-oblasti. No accessible full scan or full-region 1989 settlement table obtained. Regional totals and locality-size distributions were excluded; no Voronezh rural additions are claimed by this evidence bundle.

Run `export_extracts.py` to rebuild the CSVs from completed download manifests. The fetch scripts preserve existing raw downloads. Downloads were stopped at the bounded first-pass cutoff; no crawler is left running.
