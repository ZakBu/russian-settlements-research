## Chelyabinsk official archive directory

Primary source: Executive Committee of Chelyabinsk Regional Council of People’s Deputies, **Челябинская область. Административно-территориальное деление на 1 января 1983 года**, Chelyabinsk, 1983.

Official PDF: https://archive74.ru/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/cnpab_atd-1983.pdf

Local source: `/workspace/shared/rural_urals_history/chelyabinsk_atd1983.pdf`, 41,880,561 bytes, SHA-256 `6b1f22fd8f8a40e82b99c1698dcf27907e7885fdde2b71cb130e5e97af141e2b`.

Locators verified visually: PDF page 2 title; PDF page 3 printed pages 2–3 introduction; PDF page 4 printed page 4 control totals; PDF page 21 printed pages 38–39 Varnensky district. Each PDF interior page is a two-page spread. Introduction states directory compiled from materials of district and city executive committees and administrative state at 1 January 1983, without attribution of population to the 1979 census. Control: 1,328 rural settlements, 226 selsovets, oblast population 3,511.4 thousand and Chelyabinsk 1,076.5 thousand. Varnensky district explicitly contains 37 rural settlements, and named settlement rows have separate individual populations, alongside selsovet aggregates. This is genuinely mass own-locality material, but **the target census date is unproven**. Administrative date 1983 must not be relabeled 1979. Full OCR was stopped after date-basis check; partial OCR is evidence only, not admitted rows. Images `fast3.png`, `fast4.png`, `fast21.png`, OCR `preface3.txt` remain outside Git.

Official catalogue https://archive74.ru/spravochniki-po-administrativno-territorialnomu-deleniyu lists 1941, 1966, 1971, 1977 and 1983, with no 1980/1990/1991 directories. The archive’s announcement https://archive74.ru/news/spravochniki-administrativno-territorialnogo-deleniya confirms the same set. Searches for adjacent target directories did not locate an accessible full source. This is a bounded search result, not proof that such publications do not exist.
