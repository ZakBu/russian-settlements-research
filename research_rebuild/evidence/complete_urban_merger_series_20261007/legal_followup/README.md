## Legal findings

* Balashikha: Governor128-PG (10 June2003), clauses1–2, merges nine separate settlements and deletes their registry entries: dacha Nikolsko-Arkhangelsky, dacha Saltykovka, village Nikolsko-Arkhangelskoe, settlement Gorbovo, villages Nikolsko-Trubetskoe and Pekhra-Pokrovskoe, villages Lukino, Bezmenkovo and Novaya. Clause3 initially refuses Sovkhoza im.1Maya, Shchitnikovo and Abramtsevo. Governor180-PG (27 August2004), clauses1–2, subsequently merges those three into one settlement Balashikha. These twelve constituent observations cannot be omitted from a future complete2002 reconstruction.
* Zheleznodorozhny: Governor149-PG (28 July2004), clauses1–2, merges dacha Kupavna into one settlement Zheleznodorozhny and deletes its separate entry. Kupavna must also be included in a2002 reconstruction of2021 Balashikha. Law209/2014-OZ (30 December2014), article1, unites the cities Balashikha and Zheleznodorozhny.
* Korolev: Governor76-PG (3 April2003), clauses1–2, merges working settlements Pervomaysky and Tekstilshchik and dacha Bolshevo into Korolev and deletes their separate entries. Governor272-PG (23 December2004), clauses1–2, merges settlement Torfopredpriyatie and city Korolev into one settlement and deletes the former. Law54/2014-OZ (21 May2014), article1, unites Korolev and Yubileyny. Four additional2002 observations must therefore be included beyond the two named cities.
* Podolsk: Law103/2015-OZ (1 July2015), article1, unites Klimovsk and Podolsk; article2 abolishes Podolsky district as an administrative unit. Governor282-PG (3 July2015) separately unites working settlement Lvovsky and city Podolsk into **one populated locality**. Governor283-PG of the same date abolishes three rural administrative settlements and puts their named rural populated localities under city administration; it does **not** absorb those rural localities into the physical city. Thus municipal law81/2015-OZ alone is unsuitable for a city population reconstruction, and the rural district population cannot be added wholesale.
