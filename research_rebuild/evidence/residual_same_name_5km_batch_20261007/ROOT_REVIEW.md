# Concrete county review of the original eleven candidates

The original candidate packet is preserved. Literal name and hash checks passed but were insufficient: already accepted historical points can be the coordinates of another namesake even when the native identifier binding is correct.

Seven candidates are held for concrete county contradictions: Курилово, шоссейное, Федино, Мостовка, Юшково, Каверино, Таежный. The four eligible candidates are Юбилейный, Софийск, Горный, Карижа. Candidate statuses remain unchanged; eligibility is a recommendation for parent review.

root_admission_recommendations.csv gives per-edge reasons and source hashes. root_review_physical_county_context.csv gives literal cached source rows and file hashes. root_review_accepted_context_anchors.csv gives existing accepted source/current components around unheaded 2010 rows: Федино is bracketed within Раменский, Каверино within Ступино, Юшково within Савинский. The Kaliningrad workbook prints Гурьевский район at239, its сельские округа at243, Новомосковский сельский округ at339, Шоссейное at340. Мостовка2002 is under Кольцово / Екатеринбург, whereas the proposed2021 record belongs to Верхняя Пышма.

Population changes alone do not exclude a candidate. root_population_growth_flags.csv retains the legitimate Карижа 1→43 (+4200%) for review; zero-baseline increases are explicitly undefined ratios. County contradictions, rather than population statistics, explain the seven holds. No population values were altered.

root_eligible_candidate_identity_edges.csv contains four candidate edges. root_review_receipt.json gives their exact simulated gains against the accepted snapshot. No admission was performed.
