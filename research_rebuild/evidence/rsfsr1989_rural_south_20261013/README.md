## Sources and safeguards

Stavropol: [original official ZIP](http://stavrop.gks.ru/perepis/vpn2002/informaciya/Cis89_02.zip), [downloaded2015 archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112114215id_/http://stavrop.gks.ru/perepis/vpn2002/informaciya/Cis89_02.zip). Contains `Cis89_02.xls`, sheet `Cis89_2002`,1,154 worksheet rows. It covers736 named ruralNP and1,058,011 residents. The source explicitly adjusts1989 counts for later administrative changes. Seven affectedNP rows totaling19,500 are flagged and excluded from the usable file: Терский, Кугульта, Стародворцовский, Гражданское, Этока, Верхнестепной, Степное. Five italic post1989-created/restoredNP have0 reported1989 population and are excluded. The workbook preserves11-digit settlementOKATO codes, both-sex and separate male/female counts.

Rostov: [modern official PDF](https://61.rosstat.gov.ru/storage/mediabank/1.%D0%A7%D0%B8%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%BD%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D1%8C%2B%D0%B8%2B%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B7%D0%BC%D0%B5%D1%89%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B5%2B%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%81%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%8F%2B%D0%A0%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%B2%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B9%2B%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%BB%D0%B0%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B8.pdf), exact working archived URL is recorded in `source_manifest.json` and each CSV row. Original publisher: Ростовский областной комитет государственной статистики; title: Численность и размещение населения Ростовской области. Итоги Всероссийской переписи населения2002года; publication2004. Table9 lists2,276 ruralNP as of2002. Of these2,249 have explicit1989 values;27 show ellipsis. Twelve were urban in1989 and are excluded from rural credit (116,670 residents). Five explicit0 ruralNP are excluded from the positive usable file. Four lines omit a2002 zero sex cell; all their1989 values are explicit and verified. All2,985 explicit1989NP rows in both sources pass total=male+female. Rostov's foreword says1989 counts were not recalculated for administrative changes and warns that settlement sums need not reproduce totals. Extracted ruralNP1,229,105 is6,765 below the reported region rural total1,235,870; no residual was allocated.

## Other two regions: actual access findings

Dagestan: downloaded the Ethno-Kavkaz mainDagestan census page and all11 linked1989 district/city pages. Every inspected1989 page gives district/city totals, urbanNP and a `сёла` aggregate; there are no individually named ruralNP in these pages. Therefore zero rows were ingested. This does not establish that1989 rural sources do not exist. At least two concrete official sourcebooks are bibliographically documented: **Национальный состав населения городов, посёлков, районов и сельских населённых пунктов Дагестанской АССР по данным Всесоюзных переписей1970,1979 и1989годов**, Махачкала, Дагестанское республиканское управление статистики Госкомстата РСФСР,1990,140/141pp; and **Сельские населенные пункты Дагестанской АССР (по итогам Всесоюзной переписи1989года)**, Махачкала,1990. Bibliographic witnesses: https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Сутбук and https://aboveboard.dissercat.com/content/sovremennye-osobennosti-etnicheskogo-rasseleniya-v-respublikakh-severnogo-kavkaza . No downloadable scan or bulk1989 rural table for these books was retrieved in this bounded pass.

Krasnodar: Rosstat's2016 historical statistics conference proceedings explicitly document publication of **Сельские населенные пункты Краснодарского края** following1989 census (https://55.rosstat.gov.ru/storage/mediabank/konf-2016_sbornik.pdf). Search also surfaced a separate **Сельские населенные пункты Краснодарского края. По состоянию на1января1999**; its year is1999 and was not substituted for1989. The scholarly dissertation https://earthpapers.net/retrospektivnyy-analiz-zaseleniya-i-formirovaniya-selskogo-rasseleniya-na-territorii-krasnodarskogo-kraya provides region/district aggregates rather than usable individualNP1989 rows. No bulk1989 village scan/table was retrieved forKrasnodar in this bounded pass.
