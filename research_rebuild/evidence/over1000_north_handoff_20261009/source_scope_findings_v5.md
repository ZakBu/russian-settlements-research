# North scope and homonym resolutions (v5)

## Krasny Oktyabr, Kovrovsky target

The 2002 target is official TOM1 row 309 (PGT, 1217). A distinct PGT row 304 has 10233 and is excluded. The named own-locality source [Krasny Oktyabr (Kovrovsky district)](https://book33.ru/kovrovskij-rajon-derevni-sela-poselki/krasnyj-oktyabr-poselok-kovrovskij-rajon.html) explicitly locates the place in Kovrovsky district, reports 2002 population 1217, says it was PGT from 1945 to 2005, and says it had 944 at the 2010 census. The 2010 official-source workbook row 5385 is `посёлок Красный Октябрь` with protected published value 942; preserve 942 and its protected-quality state, despite secondary 944. The 2021 native row 2311 is in Kovrovsky/Ivanovskoye and has accepted own point 56.1058368,41.383507.

Same-name rival branches are excluded: the Gus-Khrustalny row sequence is 2002 native 819 (village table), 2010 789, 2021 740 (Gus-Khrustalny district), independently matched by its own article [Gus-Khrustalny Красный Октябрь](https://regionum.ru/vladimirskaya-oblast/gus-hrustalnyy-rayon/krasnyy-oktyabr/). This leaves the Kovrov row series as a distinct own place; the 2002 population alone is not used to resolve the identity. No population equality or boundary comparability is inferred. The 2010 native count remains 942.
