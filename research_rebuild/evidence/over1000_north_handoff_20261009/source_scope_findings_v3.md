# Source-grain findings for the remaining north handoff

* **Kryuki 2 (Pskov).** The 2002 official source file `data/raw/2002/027_25f0f7433c_02c_Pskovskaja.xls` prints separate rows `Крюки1` 22 and `Крюки2` 1530 in Ostrovsky/Berezhanskaya. The 2010 source file `data/raw/2010/012_5c339c0d0e_4._20Vologod_pskov_2010.xls` prints one `Крюки` village row (source UID `...:12525`) pop 492. 2021 raw census table lists separate Крюки 1=284 and Крюки 2=296 in Oстровский/Gorayskaya; accepted own point for 2 is 57.1763494, 28.4807042. Secondary local article says 2002 whole 1552 splits 22+1530, and that former localities were united administratively; this does not establish the 2010 row's exact membership/extent or a physical merger date. Therefore preserve 2002 individual row and 2021 individual row, hold the 2010 relationship and do not allocate 492. External witness: https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Крюки_(Островский_район) (or mirror https://regionsrf.ru/pskovskaya-oblast/ostrovskiy-rayon/kryuki/).

* **Hiidenselga (Karelia).** 2002 raw Karelia workbook `Sheet1`, row 1352, prints `Хийденсельга` as a locality label under the Lyaskelya settlement administration, but its population cells are blank. This is not a zero and does not evidence absence. 2021 source row 31481 is 1054 and has accepted own point 61.713889,30.975556 (Q4497527). No 2010 exact native row was located in selected source observations; temporal linkage is held.

* **Chalna-1 (Karelia).** The primary 2010 rural table row is 1866, and 2021 native row 31504 is 1133 in the same Prionezhsky/Garrison locality context. A different 2002 `Чална` in Pryazha is excluded; no 2002 count or point is transferred to Chalna-1. Historic name `Petrozavodsk-15` is secondary context, not proof of a separate 2002 census row.

* **Vladimirsky Lager.** The target's 2021 row is 1575 with accepted point 58.227932,29.055047. Available 2011 estimates also describe a combined urban settlement total with estimates for parts. Record 2002/2010 as composite/source-scope unknown, not zero; do not allocate totals.
