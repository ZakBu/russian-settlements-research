# New dated Wikidata population assertions, candidate layer

This supplemental layer retains 49 year-precision P1082 statements not present by statement GUID in the frozen 364,032-row secondary-history overlay. They cover 17 QIDs and 20 years (1859–2025, excluding the census years 2002, 2010, and 2021). All amounts are finite and nonnegative; no QID/year has conflicting values in this cohort. There are 48 claims with references and one without. The summed 45,346 is across unique QID/year claim values; it is not a national population total and must not be summed across years.

An independent review accepted all 17 current QID-to-selected-row bindings under a narrow rule: exact truthy P764 equals the selected native OKTMO, exact RU name/type/region, unique source row/code/QID, no native competitor or contradictory administrative-region flag, plus hash-verified full-entity physical P31 lineage. `annual_claims_reviewed_current_binding.jsonl` applies this reviewed decision; `annual_claim_candidates.jsonl` preserves the pre-review candidate status. The review receipt lists each exact selected row, native code, checks, and raw-entity batch hashes.

Both files mark every assertion `population_value_accepted=false`, `coordinate_attached=false`, and `census_identity_edge_asserted=false`. Historical scope and boundary/population comparability remain unknown. The reviewed file permits display as a secondary dated assertion attached to a currently bound place; it does not change census coverage, coordinate coverage, or temporal-linkage numerators. A separate follow-up export now supplies the reviewed current representative point as map context for those claims; its coordinate is explicitly not assigned to the historical P585 year: `current_point_context_20261005/`.
