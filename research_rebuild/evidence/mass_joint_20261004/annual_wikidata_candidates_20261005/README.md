# New dated Wikidata population assertions, candidate layer

This supplemental layer retains 49 year-precision P1082 statements not present by statement GUID in the frozen 364,032-row secondary-history overlay. They cover 17 QIDs and 20 years (1859–2025, excluding the census years 2002, 2010, and 2021). All amounts are finite and nonnegative; no QID/year has conflicting values in this cohort. There are 48 claims with references and one without. The summed 45,346 is across unique QID/year claim values; it is not a national population total and must not be summed across years.

An independent review accepted all 17 current QID-to-selected-row bindings under a narrow rule: exact truthy P764 equals the selected native OKTMO, exact RU name/type/region, unique source row/code/QID, no native competitor or contradictory administrative-region flag, plus hash-verified full-entity physical P31 lineage. `annual_claims_reviewed_current_binding.jsonl` applies this reviewed decision; `annual_claim_candidates.jsonl` preserves the pre-review candidate status. The review receipt lists each exact selected row, native code, checks, and raw-entity batch hashes.

Both files mark every assertion `population_value_accepted=false`, `coordinate_attached=false`, and `census_identity_edge_asserted=false`. Historical scope and boundary/population comparability remain unknown. The reviewed file permits display as a secondary dated assertion attached to a currently bound place; it does not change census coverage, coordinate coverage, or temporal-linkage numerators.

Two QIDs overlap the current selected residual: Арчединская (Q24484253: 5 claims, 11,985 across 1859, 1873, 1897, 1915, and 2004) and Вогулка (Q28525985: 3 claims, 622 across 1926, 2013, and 2014). Neither current row has an accepted point use or accepted historical identity path. Their claims remain candidates only.

The present-day coordinate evidence is itself unresolved for these residual nodes. The selected Tochno/DaData point for the Volgograd Арчединская row is 50.070871, 43.240109, while Wikidata P625 is 49.893056, 43.112500. A local GeoNames PPL candidate (ID 581202) is at 49.890650, 43.118250 and agrees with the Wikidata point much more closely than with the selected provider point (about 21.8 km between the two point groups). For the Perm Край Вогулка row, the selected provider point is 59.407912, 56.803917 and Wikidata P625 is 59.126500, 56.774000 (about 31.3 km apart); a GeoNames PPL candidate (ID 472914, 59.126500, 56.773200) is near the Wikidata point, while same-name GeoNames objects also exist in Sverdlovsk Oblast. This is a material point/source conflict and homonym risk, not permission to use P625 as the selected coordinate. Both points remain unaccepted.
